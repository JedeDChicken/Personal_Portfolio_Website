ESP32 RC Car

This RC Car and its camera part can be controlled through an Android phone via Bluetooth Classic. The RC Car features a camera, and users can take photos (viewed in app, via WiFi) as well as toggle the camera flash through the Android app. Attached to the camera part is a servomotor to tilt it 180° horizontally. The RC Car is powered by 4 AA batteries.

The simple Android app was developed in the MIT App Inventor, and the components and code blocks used are in the Github Repository.

Further implentation will allow for 2-way audio communication also via BT Classic, as well as miniaturize the RC Car further using 1:32 to 1:64 car models instead. Further instructions are in the Github Repository
