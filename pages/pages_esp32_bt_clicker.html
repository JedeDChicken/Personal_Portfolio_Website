ESP32 BT Clicker

This BT Clicker can be used for presentations. The buttons included are Top (<), Middle (>), and Bottom (F11). The buttons were 10kΩ pull-up and feature debounce to deal w/ noise and unintentional presses. There's also an LED to indicate BT connection. The prototype used a power bank w/ micro-USB cable as power source.

Further instructions are in the Github Repository.
